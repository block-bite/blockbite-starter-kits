### Shoe gallery prompts

- **Shoebox.** Generate one photoreal editorial product photo for a fictional independent trail-footwear brand website. A single Chalk / Black low-top trail sneaker rests diagonally inside an open natural kraft corrugated shoe box, with off-white crumpled tissue. The shoe is chalk-white mesh and white stitched overlays with striking broad jagged BLACK dry-brush embroidery zigzags, black round laces, black textile collar and heel/tongue loops, a small brushed silver circular tongue detail, sculpted white foam midsole and black lug tread. It is not a known brand. Match the product description closely. Frame the unboxing from slightly above eye level, a low three-quarter view, in lightly directional natural window light on a warm pale concrete studio table. The unmarked long FRONT cardboard face is visibly vertical and broad in the lower-middle of the photo, nearly straight-on with only a subtle slant, large enough for a wordmark to be applied later. Blank natural kraft exterior with no typography, logo, sticker, tape or fake print. The box and shoe are centred with breathing room on every edge for variable website crops. Editorial photography, natural grain and true textures, intentional hard daylight. Landscape 4:3 framing.
- **Outside.** Generate one photoreal editorial lifestyle photo for a fictional independent trail-footwear label, portrait-ish 4:3 or square composition. An adult hiker in charcoal track pants with a plain washed black windbreaker is walking up a sunny pale stone and gravel path at the edge of a windswept grassy trail. Low camera close to the ground, frame from mid-thigh down so the two shoes are the subject and the front foot is planted near center. Clearly visible Chalk / Black low-top trail sneakers: chalk white mesh and white stitched overlays with oversized bold black hand-painted jagged dry-brush zigzags sewn on the sides, black round laces, black textile collar and heel/tongue pull tabs, a discreet silver circular tongue detail, a sculpted white foam midsole and black lugged tread. The shoes must be authentic and proportionate to the person, and their striking black brushstroke pattern should be clearly visible. Natural muted greens and weathered stone, strong but believable overcast-to-sunny daylight, tactile documentary fashion photography. Keep the main shoe near image centre with extra room for responsive cropping. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no other person.
